## Demo: Cleveland Historic Circuit

Offline Champ Car / CART airport course at Burke Lakefront (Cleveland, Ohio), **1997–2007** layout (2.106 mi official, 10 turns, clockwise). Product name: **raceGPS: Cleveland Historic Circuit**.

- Citypack: `citypacks/cleveland/burke_gp_1997/`
- App: `apps/unreal-akron-beta/` — follow **BUILD.md** / **Build.bat**, Unreal Engine **5.7** (do not downgrade).
- From the menu choose **RACE** (no garage, no CARLA server).
- Grid: 1 player + 2 Chaos AI, **one lap**.
- Data checks: `pytest tests/test_cleveland_circuit.py`
- Provenance (1982 vs 1990 vs 1997): `docs/CLEVELAND_TRACK_PROVENANCE.md`
- Demo steps: `docs/CLEVELAND_DEMO.md`
